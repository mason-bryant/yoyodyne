# Milestone reached: useful self-hosting before the full management hierarchy

**Status:** Reached. Recorded by the operator, ratifiable by the architect when
the role runs.

V1 was sequenced to reach a useful self-hosting threshold — Yoyodyne able to do
real work on Yoyodyne — before implementing the entire v1 management hierarchy.
That threshold was reached, and the commitment is recorded here as met.

This was a decision about delivery order, not about what the product is for. It
was stated in [the v1 goals](../product/goals/v1-goals.md) until it was moved
here. [The product brief](../product/brief.md) is explicit that general adoption
is the aim and that self-hosting is not the finish line: Yoyodyne is finished
when someone who has never seen its internals can point it at their own
repository and get software back. Self-hosting was the scaffolding that made
building the rest possible.
